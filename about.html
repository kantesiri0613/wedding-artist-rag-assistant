Meet Harshita

Pioneering live wedding fine art across India and destination celebrations worldwide.

Harshita @livepaintingsbyharshita

Capturing Sacred Rituals, Emotion & Golden Light Live On-Site

With over 8+ years of dedicated live painting experience, Harshita was one of the early pioneers of live wedding art in India. She specializes in traditional Indian ceremonies (Varmala, Pheras, Mandap ceremonies), engagement celebrations, ballroom first dances, and Haldi magic art.

Her live performances turn weddings into interactive artistic experiences for guests while delivering a museum-grade archival oil painting with 24K gold leaf highlights that couples treasure forever.

24K Gold Leaf & Haldi Art

Specialized in embellishing canvases with pure 24K gold leaf and Haldi art.

Archival Museum Quality

High-pigment oils, Belgian linen canvas, and UV protective varnish.
