8. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)
- Q: How long does Harshita paint at the event?
  A: Harshita arrives 1.5 to 2 hours prior to start time to set up and paint background architecture, and paints live for 4 to 6 hours during the main ceremony or reception.
