Help & Logistics

Everything you need to know about booking Harshita for your wedding or event.

How far in advance should I reserve my wedding date?

We recommend reserving 4 to 12 months in advance, especially for auspicious weekend dates during peak wedding season (October to March). Dates are locked with a 30% advance deposit.

Does Harshita travel for destination weddings?

Yes! Harshita travels worldwide. Destination weddings across India (Goa, Udaipur, Jaipur, Kerala) and international locations (Dubai, Europe, Thailand) are quoted transparently covering airfare and 1-night lodging.

Do we take the painting home on the wedding night?

Yes, 85-90% of the canvas is completed live during your event so you can take it home immediately. If you select the Studio Touch-Up add-on, Harshita will take it back to the studio for fine facial detail refinements, curing, and protective UV varnishing, delivering it in 10-14 days.

What space and power does Harshita need at the venue?

A minimum 6x6 ft footprint for her wooden easel setup, access to a standard electrical outlet for professional studio lighting, and clear sightline to the stage or mandap.
