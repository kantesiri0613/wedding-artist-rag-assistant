Step-By-Step Journey

How Live Painting Works

Seamless collaboration with Harshita from booking through final delivery.

1

1. Consultation & Date Lock

We select your key moment (Varmala, Ring Exchange, First Dance, Altar), canvas size, and lock your date with a 30% advance deposit.

2

2. On-Site Setup

Harshita arrives 1.5 to 2 hours before the ceremony with easel, archival canvas, lighting, and paints to establish background decor and architectural perspective.

3

3. Live Performance

4 to 6 hours of captivating live performance. Guests watch the painting evolve and can interact with Harshita throughout the function.

4

4. Studio Polish & Delivery

Take the 85-90% done canvas home on the same night, or let Harshita apply studio facial detailing, 24K gold leaf, and protective UV varnishing before secure delivery.
