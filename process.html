How Live Painting Works

Seamless collaboration with Harshita from date booking through final museum delivery.

1

Consultation & Date Lock

Select key subjects (Varmala, first dance, stage entry), canvas dimensions, and lock date with a 30% advance deposit.

2

On-Site Setup

Harshita arrives 1.5 to 2 hours early with easel, canvas, and paints to map background architecture and decor.

3

Live Performance

4-6 hours of interactive painting. Guests watch the painting evolve live throughout the ceremony and reception.

4

Studio Polish & Delivery

Fine facial details, 24K gold leafing, and UV protective museum varnishing are cured before secure hand delivery.
